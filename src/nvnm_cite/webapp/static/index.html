Check citations

Every citation in your brief is checked for existence against the public case-citation registries on NVNM Chain — the canonical record of every Supreme Court and Eleventh Circuit citation during the pilot. A fabricated citation has no record to find.

Your document stays private.

Your brief is parsed in memory to find its citations, then discarded the moment your report is ready — never stored, never put on chain. Each citation is checked by reading NVNM Chain directly, so the verdict is the chain's answer, not ours, and anyone can re-run the same lookup. Nothing about your document goes on chain unless you choose to record a receipt.

Reading the results

Verified
A registry record exists for this citation. Existence only — it may still have been overruled, vacated, or superseded.
Not found
No record in the covering registry. Treat as presumptively fabricated until proven otherwise.
Not covered
The cited reporter or court is outside pilot coverage (us-scotus, us-ca11). No conclusion either way.
Ambiguous
The citation alone cannot place the court. Add the court parenthetical and re-check.
Unparseable
Not recognized as a citation — often an orphan short form (Id., supra) with no resolved antecedent.

One-time setup: the receipts registry does not exist yet.

Receipts are anchored into a dedicated receipts-v1 registry. It has not been created on this chain. Any wallet may create it (the creator becomes its admin); creation is a single transaction with the creation strings fixed by the record schema:

addRegistry("receipts-v1",
  "nvnm-cite filing receipts: SHA-256-keyed records of citation checks performed against the us-* registries. nvnm-cite.",
  '{"schema":"nvnm-cite-receipt/v1","spec":"cite-canonical-v1"}')

Your wallet shows the exact transaction before anything is sent. Gas on testnet is paid in wmantraUSD; an anchor costs roughly 0.005–0.01 wmantraUSD.

What the receipt asserts — and all it asserts
  1. A document with this exact SHA-256 fingerprint was citation-checked.
  2. The check ran against the named registries at the stated head block.
  3. It produced exactly these per-citation existence results.
  4. The record was signed by this address at this chain time.

It does not assert that any case supports any argument, nor that any citation is good law. Provenance, not truth.

About & status

NVNM Cite is a public registry of canonical US case citations and a receipt system for citation checks, running on NVNM Chain.

It proves provenance

A receipt proves a check happened: when, against which registries, over which exact document, attested by whom.

It never asserts truth

A verified citation exists in the registry. Whether it is good law, or supports an argument, remains the lawyer’s judgment.

Drafting stays private

Citation checks run on your machine and make zero chain or internet requests. Only a fingerprint ever leaves it — and only if you choose to record.

Anyone can audit

Registries and receipts are plaintext on a public chain. Verification is free and requires no wallet, no account, and no trust in this page.
